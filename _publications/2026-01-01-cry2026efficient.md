rationale: "Introduces a framework that for discovering of the optimal instance of a stochastic workflow net (SWN),  based on exact computation of the probabilities of each log trace to be emitted by a SWN instance. The computation of the trace probability is done via breadth-first unfolding of the rachability graph underlying the SWN and relies on memoization in order to improve the computation efficiency."
